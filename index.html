How to play

Computer

  • WASD or arrows to move
  • Mouse to turn and aim the camera (click the game first)
  • Space to jump
  • Left click or F to hit
  • Esc to free the mouse

Phone or tablet

  • Left thumb: drag to move
  • Right thumb: drag to look around
  • Tap HIT to bonk, hold it to keep swinging
  • Tap JUMP to jump

Winning

  • Bonk the other team until their HP hits 0
  • Knock out the whole other team to win
  • The winner does their special victory move

Gadgets

  • Run into a glowing box to grab a gadget
  • Use it with E, right click, or the gadget button on phones
  • Turbo Sneakers, Boing Boots, Bubble Shield, Mega Mitts, Snack Pack and Freeze Blaster

Arena

  • Jump over the spinning sweeper bar in the middle
  • Green bounce pads launch you onto crates
  • Hide behind pillars and barrels

Characters

  • Brick (Tank): 300 HP, big slow hits
  • Nova (Balanced): 200 HP
  • Zip (Fast): 150 HP, quick combos
